Implementation added
--------------------
- Exact tapered Hermite schedule q_t and p_t from the October 25 manuscript.
- Exact construction of E_t(0) and truncated E_t(t+alpha*z) factors.
- Unit-series inversion modulo each node-specific power of two.
- Coordinatewise recovery of F(0) modulo the requested precision.
- Input checks for schedule bounds, active nodes, jet order, vector dimension,
  canonical residues, unit inverses, and final 2-adic integrality.

Scope boundary
--------------
This adds a tested reference implementation of tapered Hermite recovery; it
is not the complete manuscript algorithm. The full coefficient algebra,
interpolation at all lifted residue-field points, general packing, recursive
child-network construction, global mode-count theorem, and total bit/time/
space accounting remain unimplemented. The v0.32 source archive is not in the
workspace, so this remains a standalone reference and was not integrated into
that build. No universal SAT or subexponential runtime claim is made.
